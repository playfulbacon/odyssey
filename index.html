two players · one phone · one line

HOW TO PLAY

Lay the phone flat between you, held vertically. One player sits at each end. You each own the half of the screen nearest you.

THE LINE

A paddle sits at each end of the screen. A line is stretched between them and the ball runs back and forth along it. Slide your finger anywhere on your half to move your paddle — the line swings with it. Steer the line so the ball crosses what you want to hit.

BOOST

Lift your finger to shove the ball away from you. For a moment it flies. A lift does nothing to a ball coming the other way, and if you both lift at once the two shoves cancel and the ball keeps its normal pace.

While your finger is off the screen your half of the line turns gold — and your paddle is frozen where you left it.

READING THE FIELD

Cool is safe, warm will cost you a life. Shape says it a second time — collectables are an O, obstacles an X — so you never have to pick one signal out at speed.

Among collectables the colour is just identity. Among obstacles it is not: the warm hue names the gate, the one thing that makes it safe to touch. Two obstacles that open the same way look the same.

RINGS

A violet ring means one thing everywhere: only a boosted ball takes it off. Collectable shields and obstacle armour are the same material — an unboosted ball passes straight through a ring and does nothing at all.

Each boosted pass strips ball power rings. When a collectable's last ring goes its core fills in; touch it again, boosted or not, to bank it.

The MOTE is the exception, and it is the first thing you meet: no rings, so any touch banks it. Run 1 is nothing but motes — all it asks is that you can put the line where you want it.

THE GOLD LINE

The ball only ever tells you one thing — violet means it can break a ring. The line carries the other state: the half you are not holding turns gold, and when both halves are gold the PHANTOM is open. That is why they are the same colour.

OBSTACLES

The breakable ones pay far better than any collectable:

  • SLAB and ROTOR — red. No rings, no gate, no way through.
  • BRITTLE — orange. Boost through it; three rings.
  • PULSAR — pink. Lethal while lit, inert while dark. Boost through it in a dark window.
  • PHANTOM — gold. Harmless while you are both off the glass, and one boosted pass in that same window destroys it outright.

LAUNCHING

At the start of a run, and after every lost life, both players hold their half until the bar fills.

RUNS

Every run gives you a clock and a score to beat. Clear it and spend your points on upgrades — lives, ball power, run time, boost duration — before the next one. Each run the obstacles get meaner. Miss the target, or burn every life, and the game ends.

DESKTOP KEYS

Bottom player — S hold, A D slide.
Top player — K hold, J L slide.
The mouse also works as one player. You only move while holding.

PLAYGROUND

No clock. No target. Endless lives. Pick a piece and learn it.
