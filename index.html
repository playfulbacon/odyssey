ODYSSEY

two players · one phone · one line

best — 0 ·

HOW TO PLAY

Lay the phone flat between you, held vertically. One player sits at each end. You each own the half of the screen nearest you.

THE LINE

A paddle sits at each end of the screen. A line is stretched between them and the ball runs back and forth along it. Slide your finger anywhere on your half to move your paddle — the line swings with it. Steer the line so the ball crosses what you want to hit.

BOOST

Lift your finger to shove the ball away from you. For a moment it flies. A lift does nothing to a ball coming the other way, and if you both lift at once the two shoves cancel and the ball keeps its normal pace.

While your finger is off the screen your half of the line fades — and your paddle is frozen where you left it.

READING THE FIELD

Shape tells you the family. Colour tells you the state — and the ball wears its own state, so the whole game is one rule: make the ball the same colour as the thing you want to go through.

COLLECTABLES

Every collectable is an O ringed in FORCE teal. Each pass of the ball strips one ring's worth of ball power. When the last ring goes the core fills in — touch it again to bank it.

OBSTACLES

Every obstacle is an X, wearing the colour of the ball that can get through it. The breakable ones pay far better than any collectable:

  • BRITTLE is FORCE teal — hit it with the teal, boosted ball.
  • PHANTOM is GHOST violet — hit it with both of you off the glass.
  • PULSAR is the one that changes instead of you: HAZARD red while lit, INK while dark, and any ball breaks it while it is dark.
  • SLAB and ROTOR are HAZARD red and stay that way. Nothing gets through.

LAUNCHING

At the start of a run, and after every lost life, both players hold their half until the bar fills.

RUNS

Every run gives you a clock and a score to beat. Clear it and spend your points on upgrades before the next one. Each run the obstacles get meaner. Miss the target — or burn every life — and the game ends.

DESKTOP KEYS

Bottom player — S hold, A D slide.
Top player — K hold, J L slide.
The mouse also works as one player. You only move while holding.

PLAYGROUND

No clock. No target. Endless lives. Pick a piece and learn it.
