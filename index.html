ODYSSEY

two players · one phone · one line

best — 0 ·

HOW TO PLAY

Lay the phone flat between you, held vertically. One player sits at each end. You each own the half of the screen nearest you.

THE LINE

A paddle sits at each end of the screen. A line is stretched between them and the ball runs back and forth along it. Slide your finger anywhere on your half to move your paddle — the line swings with it. Steer the line so the ball crosses what you want to hit.

BOOST

Lift your finger to shove the ball away from you. For a moment it flies. If the ball is heading toward you instead, the same lift drags it to a crawl. If you both lift at once the two shoves cancel and the ball keeps its normal pace.

While your finger is off the screen your half of the line fades — and your paddle is frozen where you left it.

COLLECTABLES

Each one wears a shield. Every pass of the ball strips ball power from it. Once the shield is gone the core is exposed — touch it again to bank it.

OBSTACLES

Touch one the wrong way and you lose a life. Some can be broken instead, and they pay far better than collectables:

  • BRITTLE — only takes damage from a boosted ball.
  • PHANTOM — only takes damage while both fingers are off the screen.
  • PULSAR — deadly while lit, breakable while dark.
  • SLAB and ROTOR — no way through. Just don't.

LAUNCHING

At the start of a run, and after every lost life, both players hold their half until the bar fills.

RUNS

Every run gives you a clock and a score to beat. Clear it and spend your points on upgrades before the next one. Each run the obstacles get meaner. Miss the target — or burn every life — and the game ends.

DESKTOP KEYS

Bottom player — S hold, A D slide.
Top player — K hold, J L slide.
The mouse also works as one player. You only move while holding.

PLAYGROUND

No clock. No target. Endless lives. Pick a piece and learn it.
