HOW TO PLAY

Lay the phone flat between you, held vertically. One player sits at each end. You each own the half of the screen nearest you.

THE LINE

A paddle sits at each end of the screen. A line is stretched between them and the ball runs back and forth along it. Slide your finger anywhere on your half to move your paddle — the line swings with it. Steer the line so the ball crosses what you want to hit.

BOOST

Lift your finger to shove the ball away from you. For a moment it flies. A lift does nothing to a ball coming the other way, and if you both lift at once the two shoves cancel and the ball keeps its normal pace.

While your finger is off the screen your half of the line breaks into faded dots — and your paddle is frozen where you left it.

READING THE FIELD

Cool is safe, warm will cost you a life. Shape says it a second time — collectables are an O, obstacles an X — so you never have to pick one signal out at speed.

Among collectables the colour is just identity. Among obstacles it is not: the warm hue names the gate, the one thing that makes it safe to touch. Two obstacles that open the same way look the same.

COLLECTABLES

There are two, and neither is a puzzle. The MOTE sits still; the DRIFTER is the same thing wandering, and pays more for the trouble of catching it. Touch either one and it is banked — no rings, no boost, no timing.

They are not where the points are. A collectable is tempo, and it is fuel for your multiplier; the obstacles are the income.

SHIELDS

Only obstacles wear a shield, and a shield is stripped by the state it is drawn in. There are two, and they look nothing alike:

  • Boost shield — violet and solid, the colour the ball turns while boosting. Only a boosted ball strips a layer.
  • Ghost shield — dotted gold, the exact treatment the line takes on once nobody is holding it. Only a ghosted line strips a layer.

Anything else passes straight through and does nothing at all. Each good pass strips ball power layers, and when the last one goes the thing inside goes with it.

THE GHOSTED LINE

The ball only ever tells you one thing — violet means it can strip a boost shield. The line carries the other key, in two steps:

  • Let go and your half breaks into faded dots straight away. That is your own state, and only yours.
  • Once you have both let go the dots turn gold and brighten. The line is now the same colour as a ghost shield, because it is now the thing that strips one.

Gold never appears on the line early. If you can see it, the phantom is open — that is the whole point of holding the colour back.

OBSTACLES

This is the whole game. The breakable ones pay several times what a collectable does, because they are the only things that can cost you:

  • SLAB and ROTOR — red. No shield, no gate, no way through.
  • BRITTLE — orange, behind a three-layer boost shield.
  • PULSAR — pink, over a boost shield. Lethal while lit, inert while dark, so boost through it in a dark window. The one piece whose gate and shield are different questions.
  • PHANTOM — gold, behind a two-layer ghost shield. Let go together and the line goes dotted gold to match; that is when a pass strips a layer. Any finger on the glass and it costs a life.

LAUNCHING

At the start of a run, and after every lost life, both players hold their half until the bar fills.

RUNS

Every run gives you a clock and a score to beat. Clear it and spend your points on upgrades — lives, ball power, run time, boost duration — before the next one. Each run the obstacles get meaner. Miss the target, or burn every life, and the game ends.
